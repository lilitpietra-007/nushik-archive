A Life at the Needle

In Dilijan, a town built into the forested hills of northern Armenia, Nushik Malkhasyan has spent decades with thread in hand, turning single knots into lace fine enough to look like frost on glass.

[Add how and from whom she learned the craft — a mother, a grandmother, a neighbour — and when she began.]

Her pieces travel hand to hand through [Dilijan and beyond], each built outward from a single point, the pattern carried in memory rather than on paper.
